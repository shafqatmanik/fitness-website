Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

How does Quiet KPI differ from traditional dashboards? ↓

Instead of requiring you to log in and check dashboards, Quiet KPI proactively delivers your most important metrics directly to your inbox. It's designed for busy founders who need insights without the context switching.

Can I customize which metrics I receive? ↓

Absolutely! You have complete control over which metrics appear in your digest, how they're calculated, and how often you receive updates. Create custom formulas and choose from hundreds of pre-built metric templates.

What integrations do you support? ↓

We integrate with 50+ popular tools including Stripe, Mixpanel, GitHub, Linear, Segment, and all major databases. Enterprise customers can request custom integrations.

Is my data secure? ↓

Yes. We use bank-level encryption, never store raw data unnecessarily, and are SOC 2 Type II certified. Your metrics are processed securely and delivered over encrypted channels.
